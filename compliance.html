Development & Compliance

This page records how the CityDrive website was planned, designed, developed and tested, together with the ethical and legal principles considered.

Development process

From brief to working website.

A user-centred, mobile-first process kept the eight-page site complete, consistent and easy for first-time renters to understand.

1

Plan

The assignment brief was converted into a content checklist covering all eight required pages, booking form fields, form confirmation flow and compliance topics. The primary audiences were defined as tourists, business travellers, families and short-term local renters.

2

Design

A clear navy-and-lime visual system was selected to communicate trust, energy and visibility. Page layouts use strong headings, short content sections, clear buttons and repeated navigation. Wireframes were used to set the order of content before styling.

3

Develop

Semantic HTML5 structures the content while one external CSS file controls colour, typography, spacing, cards, forms and responsive breakpoints. Images are stored locally, links use relative paths, and the enquiry form uses an HTML action to open the Thank You page without requiring a backend.

4

Test

Each page was checked for working navigation, correct image paths, readable content and consistent styling. Required form validation and the Thank You redirect were checked, along with keyboard focus, image alternative text and layouts at desktop, tablet and mobile widths.

Responsible web practice

Compliance considerations.

The project addresses four important areas: privacy, security, copyright and appropriate content.

P

Privacy

The enquiry form requests only information needed to respond: name, contact details, location, dates and vehicle preference. A clear consent checkbox explains the purpose. The student demonstration has no database, analytics tracker or marketing opt-in, so entered details are not stored by the site.

S

Security

Appropriate input types and required-field validation reduce accidental errors. The form avoids passwords, driver licence numbers, card numbers and other unnecessary sensitive data. A real deployment would additionally require HTTPS, secure server-side validation, access controls and a retention/deletion policy.

C

Copyright

Vehicle photographs were sourced from Unsplash for educational use under the Unsplash licence. Image filenames and this attribution provide a traceable record. Written content, branding and page layouts were created specifically for this student project; no other rental company’s logo or text is reproduced.

View the Unsplash licence

A

Censorship & appropriate content

Language and imagery are suitable for a general audience and avoid offensive or discriminatory material. Pricing is labelled as indicative, availability is not guaranteed, and safety guidance encourages lawful driving. The site does not glorify speeding, unsafe behaviour or impaired driving.

Code and file organisation

Maintainable by design.

Files use the brief’s recommended names. All pages share css/style.css, while local photographs are kept in images/. Semantic elements such as header, nav, main, section, article, form and footer improve structure for users, browsers and future developers.

Scope note: CityDrive is a fictional educational website. Prices, addresses, availability and contact information are demonstration content and do not represent a real rental service.
