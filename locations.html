First-time renter guide

Know before you go.

These simple requirements help us complete your handover quickly and safely.

Licence requirements

Drivers must present a current physical licence and meet the minimum age for the vehicle category. A licence not written in English needs an accredited English translation or International Driving Permit.

  • Minimum age: 21 for compact vehicles
  • Minimum age: 25 for SUV and premium vehicles
  • All additional drivers must be listed

Deposit and payment

A refundable security deposit is pre-authorised on a credit or eligible debit card in the main driver’s name. The amount depends on vehicle and protection choice.

  • Standard deposit: from $500
  • Card and photo ID must match the renter
  • Release times depend on your bank

Protection options

Standard damage cover is included. Plus and Premium options lower the renter’s excess. Cover has conditions and exclusions, so read the rental agreement before driving.

  • Standard excess: $4,500
  • Plus excess: $1,500
  • Premium excess: $500

Pickup and return

Arrive 15 minutes early. We verify documents, explain the controls and record the vehicle condition together. Refill to the agreed level and return on time to avoid extra fees.

  • Photograph the vehicle at pickup
  • Keep the fuel receipt
  • Remove personal items before return
Driving in Sydney

Stay safe and avoid surprises.

L

Keep left

Drive on the left and overtake on the right unless signs direct otherwise.

40

Check limits

City zones can be 40 km/h. Follow posted limits, especially near schools.

T

Plan for tolls

Sydney uses cashless toll roads. Ask about a toll tag before pickup.

P

Read parking signs

Clearways and timed zones are enforced. Check every sign before leaving the car.

This guide is general information only. Drivers are responsible for following current NSW road rules, road signs and their rental agreement.
